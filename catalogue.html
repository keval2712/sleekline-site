About Sleekline
§

Precision Engineering for Exceptional Architecture

Sleekline is a manufacturer of premium architectural metal ceiling systems and exterior solutions, established with a singular mission: to deliver products of exceptional quality that meet the exacting standards of modern architecture.

Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, we engineer and manufacture our complete range in-house — from raw material to finished, powder-coated or anodized panels. Our ISO 9001:2013 certified manufacturing process ensures every product meets consistent dimensional and quality standards across every project.

From standalone showrooms to large-scale commercial towers, Sleekline products have been installed across 500+ projects and 15+ cities across India.

ISO 9001:2013 Certified
Our manufacturing and quality management system is certified under ISO 9001:2013, ensuring consistent dimensional accuracy and product quality.
Custom Dimensions Available
Standard sizes are listed in this catalogue. Non-standard widths, lengths, and profiles are engineered and manufactured to exact project specifications.
Direct Specification Support
Our technical team works directly with architects and project specifiers — from design intent through shop drawings and site installation.
